Hawthorn Shrub

A drinking vinegar in the old country style. Wild hawthorn berries — the lone fairy tree of the Blackwater Valley hedgerows — blended with raw apple cider vinegar and cane sugar into a bright, tart-sweet cordial for sipping.

Hawthorn’s natural tannins give it structure, much like a red wine — a shrub that ages beautifully. Pour 30 ml over ice and top with cold sparkling water, and it becomes a sharp, floral drink with no alcohol.

Three ways to pour it

01

As a soft drink

Pour 30 ml over ice and top with cold sparkling water, about one to five. A sharp, floral cordial with no alcohol.

02

In a cocktail

A fine sour — shake with gin or Irish whiskey, or lengthen a glass of cold sparkling wine.

03

Over a long gin

Splash into a gin and tonic for a tart, hedgerow twist. Shake the bottle gently before pouring.
